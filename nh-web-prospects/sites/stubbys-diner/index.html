Our story

A real mom-and-pop diner.

Nothing fancy, nothing fussy. Stubby's is a cash-only, mom-and-pop diner on Old Manchester Road cooking homemade comfort food six mornings a week.

Come in for a country breakfast, a stack of pancakes or an omelet — and don't leave without trying the corn chowder with bacon and a corn muffin. It's the kind of place where the service is quick, the portions are big and you'll feel like a regular by your second visit.
