Our story

Meet Beatrice Adekoya.

Beatrice was born in Ilawe, in Ekiti State, Nigeria. Her family moved to Boston in 1998, then to Manchester in 2004 — and that same year she opened Mercy of God African Market to meet local demand for foods like pounded yam.

Today the shop on Elm Street is where neighbors find the staples of home, and a warm welcome along with them.
