Our story

Two bakers. One very big footprint.

Bigfoot Sourdough is a mother–daughter micro-bakery in Milford, New Hampshire. Everything is made by hand in small batches, and the menu follows the seasons — so there's always something new to look forward to.

Pick up a loaf at the self-serve Bread Shed, catch a seasonal drop on Instagram, or send us a custom order for porch pickup.
