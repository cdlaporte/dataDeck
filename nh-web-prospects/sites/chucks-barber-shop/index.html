Our story

A downtown Nashua institution since 1947.

Chuck's has been cutting hair on Main Street for nearly eight decades, handed down from barber to protégé to family. Same craft, same corner of downtown — and a new generation behind the chairs.

  1. 1947

    The shop opens its doors in downtown Nashua.

  2. 2008

    Sue Reynolds, protégé of owner Rino “Reno” Long, takes over as owner.

  3. 2014

    A move across Main Street to the shop's current home at 115 Main St.

  4. 2025

    Sue retires after nearly 30 years, and her daughters Dina Borges and Michelle Bracetty take over the shop.

  5. Today

    Classic cuts, walk-ins and familiar faces — carried forward by the next generation.
