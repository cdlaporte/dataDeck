1744

Nine generations of McQuestens.

McQuesten Farm has been family-owned since 1744 — that's nearly three centuries in one family. Today the ninth generation of the McQuesten family keeps it growing — planting the corn, tending the greenhouse, opening the stand, and welcoming neighbors out to pick.
